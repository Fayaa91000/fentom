Genuine feedback policy

Verified star ratings count immediately while a review is pending or published. Written comments are public only after you publish them. Hiding a review removes its stars from public averages too. Do not hide genuine criticism just because it is negative. The system records moderation decisions and reasons.

No buyer names, contact information, private order codes or payment slips are shown in this control center. Investigate suspected scams or personal data in comments before publication.
